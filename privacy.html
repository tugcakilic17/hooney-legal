Privacy & your data

Updated November 20, 2026

Hooney is for adults aged 18 and older. All companions are fictional AI characters.

What we process
Your email and sign-in session, conversations, photos, live call audio, saved memories, purchase identifiers, content reports and usage are used to deliver the service and enforce limits.

Who processes your content
OpenAI processes messages and shared photos to provide AI replies and safety checks. In voice calls, OpenAI processes your audio to transcribe it, the transcript to generate a reply, and reply and greeting text to produce the AI voice. Hooney does not store call recordings. Wiro processes image prompts and character reference photos for new image generation; fal processes image jobs submitted through the earlier image service. Supabase stores accounts, messages, media and purchase entitlements. Apple handles payment information. Our hosting provider processes API requests. We do not receive your card details.

Your choices
AI processing requires your explicit consent before chatting. Upload only media you are entitled to share. You can clear conversations, edit or erase saved memory, delete photos and report content from the conversation menu or photo viewer. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account or contacting support.

Retention and deletion
Conversations retain the last 90 days and at most 10,000 messages per account, counting your messages and AI replies separately. Saved generated and uploaded photos share a 50-photo limit, including up to 10 favorites. When full, creation and uploads stop until you delete a photo; existing saved photos are not automatically evicted. Temporary files from unsuccessful or abandoned image work are cleaned after 24 hours. Private media uses expiring access links. Deleting your account removes associated service data; records legally required for accounting or dispute resolution may need separate retention under applicable law. External providers may retain information under their own policies; contact support for deletion requests. Content may be processed outside your country.

Support
Use “Support” in Settings for data requests or concerns. Do not send highly sensitive information in chat.
