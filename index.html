HooneyAI Support

Updated November 18, 2026

HooneyAI is an AI companion app for adults aged 18 and older. All characters are fictional.

Contact us
Email tugcakilic@gmail.com for help with access, purchases, content reports, privacy or account deletion. Include your account email and, for a purchase issue, your Apple transaction identifier. Never send passwords, payment card information or API keys.

Report content
Open a conversation menu or long-press a message to report inappropriate content. Photos also have report controls. You can block a companion in the app.

Purchases
Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account. Apple handles payment refunds. Restore eligible purchases from Settings. Consumable heart balances belong to your signed-in Hooney account.

Delete your account
Open Settings in HooneyAI and choose Delete account. Deleting an account removes its service data and heart balance. It does not cancel your Apple subscription; cancel that separately in Apple Settings. If you cannot sign in, contact us from your account email.

AI and safety
AI can make mistakes. HooneyAI provides fictional entertainment and is not professional or emergency support. Explicit sexual content, exploitation and harassment are prohibited.
